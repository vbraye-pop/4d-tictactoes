How to play

  • You see one W-slice: 3 layers (Z) of 3×3 grids (Y rows, X columns).
  • Click an empty cell to place your mark. Hover shows full 4D coordinates.
  • Win by completing a cross: two 3-cell lines (any of the 40 directions, diagonals included) crossing at their middle cell.
  • 1548 crosses exist on the full board. There are 81 cells, so plan ahead.
